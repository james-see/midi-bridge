# MIDI Bridge — Demo Video Script (~45 seconds)

## Setup before recording

- Logic Pro open with a drum instrument armed on track 1
- Track 1 MIDI input set to "Logic Pro Virtual In"
- Transport playing

---

## Recording

### [0:00-0:08] Intro

> "MIDI Bridge is a Hermes Agent plugin that sends live MIDI to your DAW — notes, drums, patterns — from natural language. No piano roll, no MIDI programming."

### [0:08-0:25] The beat

Type in Hermes chat: "Play a trap beat at 140 BPM."

Show Logic Pro playing the drum pattern in real time.

> "The agent constructed the MIDI pattern and sent it to Logic Pro. I didn't program a single note."

### [0:25-0:40] Switch styles

Type: "Now play a 90 BPM boom-bap rap beat."

Show the beat changing to the slower, punchier feel.

> "Same plugin, different genre — just by asking."

### [0:40-0:45] Close

> "MIDI Bridge — because a prompt can't play piano."

---

## Tips

- Screen record with QuickTime (Cmd+Shift+5) or OBS
- The style switch mid-video is the "wow" moment — same plugin, totally different beat, just by asking
- Post on X with `@NousResearch` tagged and link the PR: https://github.com/NousResearch/hermes-agent/pull/151144
